Bara shows you people nearby who are open to talking right now. You see a made-up name, say hi, and have a short chat. If you both want to, you meet.

You stay in control.

  • A made-up name, different for everyone.

    People see a made-up name and a coloured shape, and each person sees a different one for you. Your real name and photo are never shown to others.

  • No pin on a map.

    Nobody sees your exact location.

  • Counts, not people.

    Explore shows how many people are at a place, never who they are, and hides places with too few people.

  • No awkward no’s.

    If someone doesn’t accept your request, you’re not told who passed.

  • Leave any time.

    Block, report or leave with no explanation. A person you block is never told.

  • Adults only, verified.

    Bara is for adults 18 and over, and every account is verified with a phone number.
