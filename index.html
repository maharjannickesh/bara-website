How it works

From the same room to a real conversation.

Find where people are

Explore lists places near you and how many people are there, never who they are.

Head over

See what people at a place are into, then get directions in your maps app.

See who's open to talk

Once you arrive, pick your spot. Nearby shows people within about 200 meters who are available right now.

Saying hi

Nobody gets put on the spot.

1

Send a request

Tap to say hi. If they accept, you're chatting. If they don't, nobody tells you who passed.

2

Have a short chat

Chats run on a timer, so there's no pressure to keep going. Others nearby can ask to join your table.

3

Meet if you both want to

If you both opt in, your made-up names become how you find each other in person.

Bara is for any kind of conversation: a new friend, a work contact, or just a good chat. It isn't a dating app.

Privacy and safety

Built so you stay in control.

A different name for everyone

People see a made-up name and a colored shape, and each person sees a different one.

Your location stays yours

Others only see that you're nearby. There's no pin on a map, and your exact location is never shown.

Leave any time

Block, report, or leave a chat in one tap, with no explanation. A person you block is never told.

Bara is for adults 18 and over, and every account is verified with a phone number. Read our privacy policy for what we keep and why.
