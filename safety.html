Safety

How Bara keeps people safe.

What others see

  • A made-up name and a coloured shape. Each person sees a different one for you.
  • Your real name and photo are never shown to others.
  • Nobody sees your exact location, and there is no pin on a map.
  • Explore shows how many people are at a place, never who they are, and hides places with too few people.

Saying hi

  • If someone doesn't accept your request, you're not told who passed.
  • Chats run on a timer, and adding time needs both people to agree.
  • Meeting needs both people to opt in.

If something feels wrong

  • Block, report or leave any time, with no explanation.
  • A person you block is never told.

Your data

  • The service uses your precise location to find people nearby.
  • Chats clear from the app. A private copy is kept for 90 days for trust, safety and legal reasons.
  • Bara is for adults 18 and over, and every account is verified with a phone number.
