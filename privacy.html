Privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Bara is run by Bara Inc. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it.

What other people see

Other people see a made-up name, a colored shape, your status, and your interests. They never see your real name, photo, date of birth, phone number, email, or exact location.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, date of birth, phone number, and email. We use your phone number to verify you with a one-time code, and your date of birth to confirm you're 18 or older.
  • Profile: your interests, your status, and a profile photo if you add one. Only you can see your photo.
  • Location: your precise location, so we can show you people within about 200 meters and places near you. If you allow it, this also works while the app is in the background.
  • Messages: what you send in chats.
  • Safety records: the reports you make or receive, and the people you block.
  • Device details: a token that lets us send you notifications, and an identifier for your app install.
  • Usage analytics: anonymous usage events, for example that a chat request was sent or that the Explore screen was opened, tied to a random identifier that is not your phone number, name, or alias. We never collect message contents, your location, or the people you talk to in these events.

How long we keep it

  • Location: we hold only your current location and replace it each time it updates. We don't keep a history of where you've been, with one exception: when you block someone, we keep the area name and coordinates from that moment as your own private record. It is never shown to anyone else.
  • Chats: chats clear from the app when they end. We keep a private copy of each finished chat for 90 days, for trust and safety and for legal reasons. It is never shown in the app and is never used for analytics or advertising. Access is limited to investigating incidents and responding to legal process.
  • Account details: we keep them until you delete your account.

How we use your information

We use it to run Bara, keep people safe, and understand how the app is used so we can improve it. We don't sell your data, and we don't use your messages, interests, or activity for advertising or behavioral profiling.

Who we share it with

We use service providers to run Bara, and they process data only on our behalf:

  • Amazon Web Services, for hosting and storage
  • Twilio, to send verification codes by text message
  • Apple and Google, to deliver notifications
  • PostHog, Inc. (United States), to process usage analytics

We may also share information when the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app. After you confirm with a one-time code, there's a 15-day grace period, and logging back in during that time cancels the deletion. After 15 days, your name, date of birth, phone number, and email are permanently erased.

Your choices

You can review and update your details in the app, turn location access off in your phone's settings, and contact us with any privacy question or request.

Age limit

Bara is only for people 18 and over.

Changes

If our practices change, we'll update this policy and its date.
