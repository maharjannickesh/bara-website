How can we help?

Email us at support@bara.social and a person will get back to you.

Common questions

When can I download Bara?

Bara is coming soon to iPhone and Android, launching first in the United States and Nepal.

Is Bara a dating app?

No. Bara is for any kind of conversation: a new friend, a work contact, or just a good chat.

Can people see where I am?

Nobody sees your exact location, and there is no pin on a map.

How do I report someone?

You can block, report or leave any time in the app, with no explanation.
