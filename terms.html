The service and no guarantees

A. What is Bara?

Bara is a proximity-based social application. It is designed to allow adults who are near one another, and who have each indicated that they are presently available to talk, to begin short, time-limited conversations under an alias, and, if and only if each of them separately chooses to do so, to identify one another in order to meet in person.

Bara is a communications tool. We are not a party to, and we are not responsible for, any conversation, arrangement, interaction, or meeting between users, whether it occurs on the Services or anywhere else. We do not introduce, match, recommend, vouch for, screen, endorse, or supervise any user, and we do not participate in any meeting.

B. Features of Bara

The Services currently include the following features. Sections 7, 8, and 9 describe important limits on what these features do and do not accomplish.

  • Aliases and Avatars: Other users do not see your legal name, your photograph, or your date of birth. They see a randomized alias and a stylized avatar. The alias and avatar shown to one user may differ from the alias and avatar shown to another user.
  • Time-Limited Conversations: Conversations run for a limited period. When that period ends, the conversation log displayed in the App is designed to clear.
  • Proximity-Based Discovery: The Services use your device location to indicate the general presence of other users and of places near you.
  • Group Conversations: The Services may allow several users to join a shared conversation, which we refer to as an open table.
  • Mutual Opt-In to Meet: Where two users each separately choose to meet, the Services may display to both of them a shared recognition handle so that they can identify one another in person. The Services do not disclose any user's legal name, photograph, telephone number, or other contact information to any other user. Any disclosure of that information is a voluntary act by the user who makes it, and it is made at that user's own risk.
  • Venue Hand-Off. The Services may pass a venue address to a third-party mapping or directions application on your device. Your use of any third-party application is governed by that third party’s terms, and we are not responsible for it.
